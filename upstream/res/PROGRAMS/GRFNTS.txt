@ GRFNT demonstration: every ATEXT font code, each line written in a different font, and GRMOD 4 on the last line.
@ Each line puts the font code in X for GRFNT, the text in R00, and then Y and X for ATEXT. Each DROP𝑥 takes one value off the stack.
@ Y is the bottom of the first line of the text. Codes 10 to 23 are in the left column, 30 to 41 in the right column.
; Jaco Mostert  GRFNT demonstration

LBL 'GRFNTS'
  # GRFNT examples
  CLLCD

  10 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '10 tiny Ag19'       STO 00 DROP𝑥  210   2 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  20 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '20 standard Ag19'   STO 00 DROP𝑥  186   2 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  21 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '21 compressed Ag19' STO 00 DROP𝑥  162   2 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  22 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '22 bold Ag19'       STO 00 DROP𝑥  138   2 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  23 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '23 enlarged Ag19'   STO 00 DROP𝑥  102   2 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥

  30 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '30 Ag19'            STO 00 DROP𝑥  184 210 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  31 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '31 Ag19'            STO 00 DROP𝑥  156 210 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  32 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '32 Ag19'            STO 00 DROP𝑥  136 210 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  40 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '40 Ag19'            STO 00 DROP𝑥  100 210 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥
  41 GRFNT DROP𝑥   '41 Ag19'            STO 00 DROP𝑥   72 210 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥

@ GRMOD 4 clears the glyph pixels on a filled box: white text on black.
  4 GRMOD DROP𝑥  22 GRFNT DROP𝑥  'GRMOD 4 with GRFNT 22' STO 00 DROP𝑥  40 2 ATEXT 00 DROP𝑥 DROP𝑥

@ GRMOD and GRFNT return to their defaults, 0 and 20.
  0 GRMOD DROP𝑥  20 GRFNT DROP𝑥

  # Waits for a key
  PAUSE 99
RTN
END
