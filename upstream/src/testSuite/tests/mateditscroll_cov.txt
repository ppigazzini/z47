;*************************************************************
;** Coverage: the matrix editor's column scroll offset      **
;** across a reshape (showRealMatrix, showComplexMatrix).   **
;** Each case opens the editor on the 16x1 vector in X,     **
;** presses the down arrow NN times and reports one number. **
;** FARG=0NN scrolled, 1NN the offset after M.COL+1 widens  **
;** to two columns, 2NN whether it is past the new shape,   **
;** 3NN the offset when widened to the offset itself, 4NN   **
;** whether that made the two equal. 1NN and 3NN are the    **
;** gate: unbounded they kill the run, not just fail.       **
;*************************************************************

; Real 16x1: sixteen columns on screen
;=======================================
